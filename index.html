Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know About ASR

ASR Degree College is permanently affiliated to Vikrama Simhapuri University (VSU), Nellore. All undergraduate degree certificates are officially awarded by VSU and recognized by the UGC and Government of Andhra Pradesh.

Yes. All eligible students qualifying under Government of Andhra Pradesh welfare guidelines receive full tuition fee reimbursement under JVD. Our administrative office provides complete documentation support.

Yes. We have a dedicated, strictly secured on-campus Girls Hostel equipped with 24/7 CCTV surveillance, biometric security, resident female wardens, RO purified drinking water, and hygienic steam kitchen meals.
