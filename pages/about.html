Home / About ASR Degree College

Where Knowledge Meets Opportunity in Atmakur

Founded to deliver world-class academic rigour, technological fluency, and character-building education to aspiring students of Andhra Pradesh.

01 / Our Purpose

Building intellectual confidence and practical skills from the very first semester.

ASR Degree College, situated at Priyadarshini Nagar, Atmakur, is recognized under the regulatory framework of the Andhra Pradesh State Council of Higher Education (APSCHE) and permanently affiliated to Vikrama Simhapuri University, Nellore.

We firmly believe that geographical location should never limit a student’s ambition. By bringing top-tier faculty, modern computer laboratories, data science curriculum, and structured corporate placement training to Atmakur, we have enabled hundreds of local youth to secure lucrative careers in global IT and corporate companies.

Institutional Vision

Our Vision

To be a beacon of academic excellence and holistic student development in the Rayalaseema and Coastal Andhra regions—transforming undergraduate students into industry-ready software developers, business analysts, scientists, and responsible leaders.

Action Roadmap

Our Mission

To offer student-centric, technology-oriented undergraduate education; to cultivate analytical reasoning through hands-on lab experiments; to foster ethical citizenship; and to guarantee 100% placement readiness through continuous Campus Recruitment Training (CRT).

02 / Why Choose ASR

The Four Pillars of the ASR Educational Advantage

💻

Industry-Aligned Curriculum

Degrees in Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, and Business Analytics paired with Python, Java, and SQL coding practice.

🎯

Dedicated Placement Cell

Highest package ₹6.5 LPA. Regular aptitude, group discussion, JAM, and mock interview drills starting in Semester 1.

🛡

Safe Environment & Hostel

Strict anti-ragging discipline, 24/7 CCTV surveillance, and dedicated on-campus girls hostel with resident warden.
