Confidence is Trained: Practical CRT & Industry Internships

Beyond textbook lectures, we equip every undergraduate with competitive programming skills, corporate English fluency, and real-time internship exposure.

01 / Skill Architecture

Transforming students into confident problem solvers.

Campus Recruitment Training (CRT) at ASR Degree College is an integrated part of our academic timetable, not an afterthought. Every week, students engage in dedicated CRT hours covering quantitative ability, logical reasoning, data interpretation, verbal ability, and coding challenges.

Our internal trainers collaborate with IT professionals from Hyderabad, Bengaluru, and Chennai to conduct hackathons, coding sprints in Python and Java, and rigorous mock interview simulations.

Core Training Tracks

Four Specialized Skill Tracks for Every Student

⌨

Full-Stack Coding Bootcamps

Hands-on coding in Python, Java, JavaScript, React, SQL, and Git. Students build live web apps and publish portfolios online.

🎙

Digital Language Lab & JAM

Eliminate mother-tongue influence (MTI), master corporate voice modulation, and develop spontaneous public speaking skills through daily JAM sessions.

📈

Aptitude & Speed Math

Shortcut techniques for quantitative aptitude, analytical reasoning, and data interpretation required by TCS NQT, Wipro, and bank exams.

🏢

Corporate Internships

Mandatory summer internship program connecting students with software firms, accounting firms, and food processing plants for live workplace experience.

Value-Added Badges

Globally Recognized Certifications

We guide students to complete industry-recognized certifications through Swayam/NPTEL, Spoken Tutorial (IIT Bombay), Python Institute, and Google Digital Garage before their final year placement drives.
