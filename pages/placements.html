Our Methodology

The 4-Tier Campus Recruitment Training (CRT) Model

SEMESTERS 1 & 2

Foundational Aptitude

Speed mathematics, mental arithmetic, vocabulary building, and basic computer fundamentals to set strong core habits.

SEMESTER 3

Communication & JAM

Just-A-Minute (JAM) speeches, active group discussions, interpersonal etiquette, and corporate presentation drills.

SEMESTERS 4 & 5

Technical Coding Bootcamps

Intensive hands-on programming in Python, Java, SQL queries, web development, and mini-projects hosted on GitHub.

SEMESTER 6

Mock HR Interviews & Drives

Full-scale simulations with external corporate HR panelists, resume optimization, and on-campus recruitment drives.
