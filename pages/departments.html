Centers of Specialized Learning and Practical Research

Our academic departments bring together subject matter specialists, dedicated computer and science laboratories, and high-impact industry certifications.

Department 01

Department of Computer Science & Applications

Programmes: BCA, BCA-AI, BCA-DS, B.Sc-CS

The flagship technical department at ASR Degree College, educating over 400+ students in software development, cloud technologies, machine learning, and relational databases. Equipped with two fully air-conditioned computer laboratories featuring 120+ modern Intel Core workstations.

💻 Dedicated Labs: AI & Data Analytics Lab, High-Speed Web Development Center
🚀 Primary Tech Stack: Python, Java, React, SQL, TensorFlow, PowerBI, Linux
120+ Workstations · AC Labs
Department 02

Department of Commerce & Management Studies

Programmes: BBA-BA, B.Com-CA

Prepares future business executives, accountants, and marketing analysts. The department focuses on the convergence of financial acumen and modern analytical tooling, providing hands-on training in GST compliance, Tally Prime with computerized accounting, and corporate business intelligence.

📊 Lab Facilities: Financial Modeling & Tally Certification Lab
💼 Career Outcomes: Business Analyst, Financial Associate, Tax Consultant, Banker
Tally & GST Certified
Department 03

Department of Life Sciences & Food Technology

Programmes: B.Sc Food Science & Technology

Dedicated to addressing industrial food safety, processing quality, and agricultural biotechnology. Students study food microbiology, chemical preservation, nutrition evaluation, and industrial dairy/agro-processing in state-of-the-art wet and dry science laboratories.

🔬 Lab Facilities: Food Microbiology, Biochemistry & Quality Analysis Lab
🧪 Key Industry Tie-ups: Food processing plants, dairy units, and FSSAI testing labs
FSSAI Protocol Lab

Department of Humanities & CRT Career Cell

🎯 Core Training: Aptitude, Verbal Ability, Resume Engineering, GD
