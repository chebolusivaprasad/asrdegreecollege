Centers of Specialized Learning and Practical Research

Explore our 7 specialized academic departments bringing together subject matter specialists, dedicated computer and science laboratories, and high-impact industry certifications.

Department 01

Department of Computer Applications

Programme: BCA (Bachelor of Computer Applications)

The flagship software engineering department at ASR Degree College, preparing students in core and advanced programming, web application development, database architecture, and cloud operations. Features modern high-performance workstations with gigabit connectivity.

💻 Dedicated Labs: Full-Stack Web Center & Oracle Database Lab (120+ PCs)
🚀 Primary Tech Stack: Java, Python, C++, React.js, MySQL, Spring Boot, Linux
120+ Workstations · AC Labs
Department 02

Department of Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning

Programme: BCA — Artificial Intelligence

Specialized department dedicated to modern artificial intelligence, deep neural networks, computer vision, natural language processing, and generative AI systems. Equipped with GPU-accelerated computing nodes for heavy model training.

🤖 Lab Facilities: GPU-Accelerated AI Lab & Computer Vision Studio
⚡ Tools & Frameworks: TensorFlow, PyTorch, Scikit-Learn, OpenCV, LangChain, HuggingFace
AI & Neural Networks Lab
Department 03

Department of Data Science & Big Data Analytics

Programme: BCA — Data Science

Focuses on mathematical statistics, predictive modeling, big data pipelines, and visual storytelling through business intelligence dashboards. Prepares students for quantitative roles in data engineering and statistical analysis.

📊 Lab Facilities: Big Data Analytics Lab & BI Visualization Center
📈 Core Technology: Python (Pandas, NumPy), R, SQL, PowerBI, Tableau, Apache Spark
Data Analytics Center
Department 04

Department of Computer Science (Physical Sciences)

Programme: B.Sc — Computer Science

Combines computational theory, hardware architectures, discrete mathematics, and microprocessor assembly with core software engineering. Designed for students pursuing a strong foundation in scientific computing and research.

⚙️ Lab Facilities: Systems Programming & Microprocessor Architecture Lab
🔬 Academic Focus: Algorithms, Operating Systems, Computer Architecture, C/C++, Linux
Theory & Systems Lab
Department 05

Department of Business Administration & Analytics

Programme: BBA — Business Analytics

Nurtures future business executives, management consultants, and operations analysts through executive case studies, market analytics, financial modeling, and corporate leadership workshops.

💼 Facilities: Executive Boardroom & Business Intelligence Simulator
📈 Career Tracks: Business Analyst, Product Associate, Operations Consultant, HR Executive
Management & Analytics
Department 06

Department of Commerce & Computer Applications

Programme: B.Com — Computer Applications

Provides a powerful combination of financial accounting, taxation principles, GST filing compliance, and computerized accounting software. Trains students to handle corporate audits, banking operations, and tax advisory.

📊 Lab Facilities: Tally Prime Certification Lab & GST Filing Center
💼 Skill Certifications: Tally Prime Gold, GST Compliance, Advanced Excel & Financial Modeling
Tally & GST Certified
Department 07

Department of Food Science & Technology

Programme: B.Sc — Food Science & Technology

Dedicated to industrial food preservation, microbiology safety standards, nutritional quality testing, and agro-processing technology. Students gain practical experience following rigorous FSSAI standards in fully equipped science labs.

🔬 Lab Facilities: Food Microbiology, Biochemistry & Quality Analysis Lab
🧪 Key Industry Tie-ups: Agro-processing units, dairy plants, and FSSAI testing centers
FSSAI Protocol Lab
