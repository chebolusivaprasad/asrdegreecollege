01 / The Admissions Journey

Four Simple Steps to Join ASR Degree College

Step 1: Submit Online Enquiry or Visit Campus Fill the form or walk into our admissions cell in Atmakur (Opp. SBI ATM) with your 10th and Inter marks memos.
Step 2: Course & Career Counseling Meet with our senior faculty deans to understand syllabus options, coding training, and placement opportunities.
Step 3: Document Verification & JVD Registration Submit Aadhaar, SSC & Inter Memo, Transfer Certificate (TC), Caste/Income certificate for JVD fee reimbursement.
Step 4: Formal University Enrollment Receive official enrollment confirmation, college ID card, bus pass, and hostel allotment.
🎓 Govt. of AP Fee Reimbursement (JVD Scheme):

Eligible students under Jagananna Vidya Deevena (JVD) and Vasathi Deevena can pursue degree education with 100% full tuition fee assistance credited through the government welfare portal. Our office provides full documentation support.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What documents are required for admission?

SSC Marks Memo, Intermediate Marks Memo/Pass Certificate, Transfer Certificate (TC), Study/Conduct certificates (6th to Inter), Aadhaar Card copies, Caste & Income certificates (for fee reimbursement), and 4 passport-size photographs.

Is bus transportation available from my town?

Yes. ASR operates dedicated buses across Atmakur, Marripadu, Rapur, Kaluvoya, Ananthasagaram, Badvel, and surrounding villages. Safe pickup and drop is guaranteed for all day scholars.

Is the Girls Hostel located inside the campus?

Yes. We have a dedicated, strictly secured Girls Hostel with resident female wardens, 24/7 CCTV surveillance, nutritious hygienic meals, and medical support.

When do classes begin for the 2026–27 batch?

Classes commence strictly as per the academic calendar issued by Vikrama Simhapuri University. Orientation and bridge courses start in early August.
