Chairman's Message

Founder & Chairman / Correspondent, ASR Educational Society

“Education is the greatest catalyst for human dignity and economic upliftment. Our vision at ASR has always been clear: provide the youth of Atmakur with the same technological opportunities and career pathways enjoyed by students in top metropolitan cities.”

When we established ASR Degree College, our goal was to bridge the gap between traditional textbook education and modern corporate demands. Today, with cutting-edge degree programmes in Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, and Business Analytics, and our graduates working in multinational tech giants, I take immense pride in our students' accomplishments.
