World-Class Infrastructure Built for Focus and Growth

Experience an academic environment designed for the everyday rhythm of deep study, practical lab experimentation, safety, and well-being in Atmakur.

Technical Labs

Air-Conditioned Computer Labs

Two state-of-the-art computer laboratories featuring 120+ modern Intel Core i5/i7 workstations with dual monitors, high-speed optical fiber internet, and continuous power generator backup.

  • Gigabit fiber internet connection
  • Preloaded Python, Java, R, SQL, and Android developer SDKs
  • Open beyond scheduled college hours for student practice
Student Residence

Dedicated Girls Hostel

Located on-campus to provide female students from outside Atmakur a safe, disciplined, and comfortable home with 24/7 CCTV surveillance, biometric access, and resident female wardens.

  • Spacious, well-ventilated furnished rooms
  • Hygienic steam kitchen serving nutritious South Indian meals
  • Mandatory supervised evening study hours and medical aid
Daily Commute

Extensive College Bus Fleet

A fleet of well-maintained college buses connecting Atmakur to all surrounding mandals and towns, ensuring punctual, safe, and stress-free daily transit for our day scholars.

  • Routes covering Marripadu, Rapur, Kaluvoya, Ananthasagaram, Badvel
  • Trained drivers with real-time GPS tracking
  • Subsidized student transport passes
Research & Study

Central Digital Library

Our quiet sanctuary of knowledge houses over 5,000+ academic volumes, reference textbooks, international research journals, competitive exam materials, and Delnet digital access.

  • Dedicated digital reading room with internet terminals
  • Comprehensive material for Bank PO, RRB, SSC, and APPSC exams
  • National newspapers, business magazines, and periodicals
Scientific Research

Food Technology & Science Labs

Equipped with modern microscopes, autoclaves, incubators, spectrophotometers, and food preservation test kits for hands-on biochemical and microbiological research.

  • Industrial standard food processing testing kits
  • Strict laboratory safety protocols and protective gear
  • Individual experiment workbenches for all students
Fitness & Culture

Sports Grounds & Auditorium

Physical fitness and cultural expression are integral to student vitality. Our campus features a cricket pitch, volleyball court, badminton nets, and a 300-seat audio-visual seminar auditorium.

  • Annual Inter-Departmental Sports Meet and trophies
  • Indoor games: Chess, Carroms, and Table Tennis
  • Acoustically treated auditorium for guest lectures and cultural fests

Want to see our campus in person?

Parents and prospective students are welcome to visit our campus, inspect our computer labs, interact with faculty, and tour our girls hostel.
