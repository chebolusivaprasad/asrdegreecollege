The Campus Has a Pulse: Workshops, Fests & Sports

Student life at ASR Degree College extends far beyond lectures. We celebrate technical innovation, cultural vitality, and leadership through an active yearly calendar.

18 SEP 2026
Academic Orientation

Annual Academic Induction & Freshers' Welcome

A ceremonial beginning welcoming the incoming 2026–27 batch. Orientation by Principal and HODs introducing the VSU semester system, coding labs, and mentor allocations.

04 SEP 2026
Technical Bootcamp

National CodeSprint & Python Hackathon

A 24-hour hands-on coding challenge where BCA and B.Sc Computer Science students build full-stack web applications, AI chat assistants, and data analytics dashboards.

28 AUG 2026
Social Outreach

NSS Mega Blood Donation & Tree Plantation Drive

Organized by the ASR National Service Scheme (NSS) unit in partnership with local health centers in Atmakur, instilling social responsibility and community welfare.

15 AUG 2026
National Day

Independence Day & Academic Merit Awards

Flag hoisting ceremony followed by the distribution of institutional scholarships and merit certificates to VSU semester university toppers by our Chairman.
